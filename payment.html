How would you like to pay?

Step 3 of 4

Pay with bKash

A payment prompt appears in your bKash app. Approve it within two minutes.

Use the number registered with bKash. The wallet prompt is simulated in this concept.

Pay with Nagad

Open Nagad and approve the prompt. You will return here automatically.

Pay by card

Visa, Mastercard and American Express are accepted. 3-D Secure may ask for an OTP.

Pay by bank transfer

You will be sent to your bank's secure page to approve the transfer.

Demo details are prefilled so the flow can be completed. No payment is taken and no details are stored.

Paying at the station?

Counter payment is not part of this concept. A live build would hold the seat for 30 minutes and release it if the counter does not confirm.
