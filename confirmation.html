Before you go

Three small things.

  1. 01

    Arrive at Kamalapur 30 minutes before departure. Platforms are announced 20 minutes ahead.

  2. 02

    Bring the photo ID used for the booking. The name on the ticket must match.

  3. 03

    Show the PNR or gate code when the conductor reaches your coach. No printout required.

Wheelchair or extra help?

Ask the station desk or call 13107 at least four hours before departure.
