Common questions

The short answers.

Filtered live as you type

How long is a seat held?
Ten minutes from the moment you continue from the seat map. The timer is visible on the booking and payment screens. If it runs out, the seats are released and you choose again.
Can I book without creating an account?
Yes. Search, choose a seat and pay as a guest. An account only adds convenience: saved passengers, payment methods, and a single place for every PNR.
When do seats release for a date?
This concept shows seats releasing ten days ahead. A live build would follow the railway's own advance booking window, including Eid and holiday rules.
How do I cancel and get a refund?
Open the trip from My trips and choose cancel. More than 24 hours before departure, 90% of the fare starts back to the original payment method. Three working days is the usual arrival time.
Can I change the passenger name?
Name changes are not part of this concept. In a live product, they would depend on the railway's rules and the time left before departure. For now, cancel and rebook.
Which wallets and cards do you accept?
bKash, Nagad, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and bank transfer from Sonali, BRAC, City, Dutch-Bangla and Islami Bank. The checkout is a design prototype, so nothing is charged.
What if the train is late?
Check the live status card on this page or in your dashboard. Platform changes are sent by SMS when you opt in. If the railway cancels a service, a full refund would start automatically.
Is the ticket valid on my phone?
Yes. The PNR and gate code are enough. Keep a screenshot offline for peace of mind, and carry the photo ID used in the booking.
Can I book a women's coach seat?
Every intercity carries a Mahila compartment. In a live build, the seat map would switch to that coach when the filter is on. This concept shows the control and marks the feature on the map.
Wheelchair or extra help at the station?
Call 13107 at least four hours before departure. The station desk can arrange a wheelchair, boarding help, and a lower berth where available.

The fine print

Small terms, plainly.

This is a design concept, not a railway service. Nothing here books a real seat or charges money.

  • 01

    Tickets and fares are sample data for four corridors. They do not represent Bangladesh Railway inventory or pricing.

  • 02

    A live booking would follow the railway's advance booking window, passenger ID rules and cancellation policy. The numbers here are illustrative.

  • 03

    Payments, refunds and PNRs are simulated in the browser. No card, wallet or bank details are transmitted or stored.

  • 04

    Independent concept, not affiliated with Bangladesh Railway. “tikit” is a working name for this design.
